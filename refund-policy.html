Scope This policy applies to store subscriptions to Revo, an AI assistant that answers a store's customers on WhatsApp. The service is provided by Rival bint Faisal Al Abdullah. 2. Subscription and renewal First month: SAR 400, covering the assistant's setup and the first month's subscription. From the second month: SAR 200 per month, paid in advance for each month. Maintenance is included: updates to the store's information and prices and technical support, up to two updates a month. We may run limited offers on the first month; their terms are stated at the time of the offer. The subscription renews automatically every month on the same date unless the store owner asks to cancel. WhatsApp message fees charged by Meta are paid to Meta directly from the store's own account and are not part of the subscription. 3. Free trial The trial lasts 3 days, starting on the day the assistant goes live on the store's number. No payment is required during the trial, and it can be stopped at any time during it at no cost. After the trial, the paid subscription starts when the first month is paid. 4. Cancellation Cancellation can be requested at any time, with no contract or minimum term. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month: the assistant keeps working until the last day of that month, then the subscription does not renew and nothing more is charged. Send cancellation requests to the email in the Contact section, or through a WhatsApp chat with the Revo team. 5. Refunds The current month is not refunded when cancellation is requested partway through it, because the service has been provided and keeps working until the end of the paid month. The following cases are exceptions: Duplicate charge or wrong amount: the excess amount is refunded in full once verified. Outage caused by us: if the assistant stops answering for more than 72 consecutive hours because of a fault in Revo's systems, the store owner receives free days equal to the outage, added to the subscription. This does not cover interruptions outside Revo's control, such as Meta or WhatsApp outages, the store's number or Meta account being restricted, or non-payment. Amounts due are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds may take several business days depending on the bank. 6. Non-payment If payment is not made on the renewal date, the assistant stops after 24 hours, and customer chats go to the store owner to answer personally. The assistant resumes once payment is made, with no reactivation fee. 7. Price changes If the subscription price changes, we notify the store owner at least 30 days before the new price applies, and it never applies to a month already paid. 8. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time; the new effective date appears at the top of this page. 9.
